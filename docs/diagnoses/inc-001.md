# inc-001 · checkout-api 503s (example diagnosis)

Deploy d-0142 lowered `DB_POOL_MAX` from 20 to 5. When traffic rose at 14:02 the pool saturated and requests timed out waiting for a connection (503).
Evidence: L0002 ("config loaded"), L0021 ("timeout acquiring db connection from pool after 2000ms"). Ruled out: PSP latency (flat), Postgres overload (5 of 200 connections).
Proposed (not executed): runbook `db-pool-exhaustion` step A, a PR restoring `DB_POOL_MAX=20`, approved by the named on-call.
